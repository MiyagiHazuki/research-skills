# Examples — Academic Writing

Worked examples for [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Each example stays in one language, including its change log.

## English

Original:

> The utilization of a state-of-the-art framework—which has been demonstrated to facilitate the analysis—is, in fact, necessary in order to achieve the desired outcome, despite the fact that it may be complex.

Rewritten:

> Using an advanced framework is necessary to achieve the desired outcome, even though it may be complex. This framework has been shown to help the analysis.

Changes:

- Dashes: 1 paired insertion; became an independent sentence.
- Hyphens: state-of-the-art → advanced.
- Vocabulary: utilization → using; demonstrated → shown; facilitate → help; in order to → to; despite the fact that → even though.
- Sentences: one long compound sentence split into two simple sentences.

## 中文

改写前：

> 本研究——基于对大量数据的系统分析——发现，该模型在诸多方面均能够对现有方法实现显著的性能提升，尽管其在某些特定条件下仍然存在着一定的局限性。

改写后：

> 本研究基于对大量数据的系统分析。研究发现，该模型在许多方面都能显著提升现有方法的性能。但它在某些特定条件下仍有局限。

修改说明：

- 破折号：1 处成对插入语，改为前置状语并单独成句。
- 词汇：诸多 → 许多；实现了显著的性能提升 → 显著提升了……的性能；仍然存在着一定的局限性 → 仍有局限。
- 句子：1 个长句拆为 3 个短句。

## What not to change

Keep en dashes in ranges and hyphens in standard technical terms:

> Samples were collected in 2010–2020 and analyzed with a non-parametric t-test.

- `2010–2020` is a numeric range (en dash) — keep.
- `non-parametric` and `t-test` are standard statistical terms — keep.
